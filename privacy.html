This Privacy Policy explains how TTH Development (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use TTH's Assistant (“the Bot”) on Discord.

1. Information the Bot may process

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs and server (guild) IDs, used to associate your commands and saved data with you and the relevant server.
  • Content you provide: note, task, reminder text, AI prompts, and other text entered into Bot commands.
  • Reminder details: reminder dates, times, and delivery information needed to send reminders.
  • Server configuration: selected welcome/leave channels, message templates, role IDs, and related settings configured by server administrators.
  • Operational information: command and error information that may appear in the Bot’s runtime logs for troubleshooting and security.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide requested Bot features, retrieve your notes and tasks, schedule reminders, configure server-specific features, respond to commands, maintain security, and diagnose errors.

3. AI features and Google Gemini

When you use an AI command, the prompt you submit and the context needed to generate a response are sent to Google Gemini through Google’s API. Do not submit sensitive personal information, passwords, payment details, API keys, or authentication tokens. Google processes information under its own terms and privacy documentation. We do not control Google’s independent processing.

4. Storage and retention

In the current implementation, saved notes, tasks, reminders, and server configuration are stored by the Bot in its local application data store on the machine or hosting environment running the Bot. AI conversation context and usage counters may be held temporarily in the Bot’s memory while it runs. Operational logs may also be generated.

Saved information may remain in the local data store until it is deleted through an available Bot command or a deletion request is handled by the operator. We do not state a fixed automatic deletion period. If the Bot is moved, reset, or its storage is deleted, saved features may be affected.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as needed to operate the Bot: with Discord to receive commands and deliver responses, and with Google Gemini when you use AI features. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or our rights.

6. Access and deletion requests

You may delete individual notes and tasks using the relevant Bot commands where available. For other information or a request to delete data associated with your Discord user ID or server, contact the TTH Development team through our official Discord server. Ask for a private support channel and identify the Bot feature and Discord account/server concerned. We may need to verify that you control the relevant account or server before acting. Do not post sensitive information in a public channel.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the current project to limit access to stored information. No internet-connected service or local storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Server administrators should carefully manage Bot permissions and access to the machine and files where the Bot runs.

8. Children

The Bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or Discord’s age requirements. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact us through the support server.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Bot’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact TTH Development through the official TTH Discord server. Ask the server team for a private support channel. The Discord server is our current contact route; it is not a dedicated privacy-request portal.

Important: This draft describes the currently understood bot design: local storage for notes/tasks/reminders and server settings, temporary in-memory AI context, Discord, and Google Gemini API. Before publishing, verify every statement against the exact code you deploy. If you add music providers, analytics, databases, hosting vendors, or new data collection, update this Policy first. This document is not legal advice.
